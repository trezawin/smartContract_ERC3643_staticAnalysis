High

1. On-chain registration of licensed persons missing
The contract does not implement the required 'identityRegistry()' function in 'idr' to support on-chain registration of licensed persons, as evidenced by the absence of 'registerIdentity(address,address,uint16[])' in the 'idr' interface. This contravenes SFO Cap.571 §53ZRH(1), which mandates maintaining an on-chain register of licensed entities. The lack of this functionality impairs regulatory oversight, increases the risk of unverified or unregistered entities operating, and hampers auditability.
Compliance Ref: SFO (Cap.571) §53ZRH(1)
Evidence Paths:
  • Contract ABI inspection: 'idr' interface missing 'registerIdentity(address,address,uint16[])' function
Recommendation: Implement the 'registerIdentity' function within the 'idr' interface and ensure it is invoked during the licensing process. Develop comprehensive unit and integration tests to verify registration logic, and establish operational run-books for ongoing registration management. Maintain detailed event logs for all registration activities to support audit trail requirements.
